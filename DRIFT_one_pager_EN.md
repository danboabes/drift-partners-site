## Programmes rarely fail because people stop working.
**They fail when they stop moving in the same direction.**

Work continues. Effort increases. Decisions are made. And still — things don't move forward. Deadlines slip. Problems appear too late. Leaders lose real visibility.

You feel it. Your people feel it. But you cannot fully see it.

Drift is a loss of direction at the system level. The organisation no longer operates on a shared reality. Activity replaces alignment.

---

## How we work

Restoring direction in a drifting programme is unfashionable work. It requires four things, in this order, and each one is harder than the next.

### 01 Make the system visible
We look at what is actually happening — not what is reported. We create conditions where people tell us what they actually see. We observe how things really work.

### 02 Identify what breaks alignment
Every drift is different. We isolate what drives it — not the symptoms. Five families of mechanisms: technical blind spots, capability gaps, decision breakdowns, structural contradictions, information distortion.

### 03 Restore direction through decisions
We work with leadership to define what must change, remove contradictions, and make decisions the system can actually follow. We then transfer the work to the teams — through workshops and mentoring — so the new direction lives in the people who execute it, not only in a steering deck.

### 04 Anchor it in the system
Without reinforcement, drift returns. Quarterly review of whether each prior decision held, replaced, or drifted. Light re-sampling of the signals. Early intervention on new mechanisms.

---

## What changes

This is not automatic. It happens when leadership is willing to look at what has been avoided, and when the work of restoring direction is done — by us and by you.

→ Problems begin to surface earlier — before they become crises.

→ Decisions begin to hold — when they are actually decisions.

→ Teams begin to act in the same direction.

→ Leaders see what they could not see before.

**Direction is restored.**

## A note on the work

DRIFT Partners works with cross-border programmes — distributed engineering and delivery, transitions, post-merger integrations, multi-site operations — where leadership has lost real visibility into how the programme actually operates.

We do not run change programmes. We do not deliver decks. We do not redraw RACI charts. We work with programme leadership in a senior, embedded posture for a defined period, against a defined deliverable.

We do not promise easy. We do not sell certainty. We sell visibility — and the work that follows from it.

We are a small team. We will stay small. The success indicator is not how long we stay — it is how cleanly we leave.
